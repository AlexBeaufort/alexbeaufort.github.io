About Me

As a computer engineering student at the University of Central Florida graduating in August 2025, I am passionate about solving complex challenges through technology. My diverse experiences span software integration, web and mobile development, and hands-on research, where I've contributed to impactful projects like designing an exoskeleton for resistance training and developing data-driven tools for strategic decision-making.

I thrive at the intersection of embedded systems, software engineering, and problem-solving. My internships at General Electric Vernova and U.S. Hunger have allowed me to tackle real-world challenges, from optimizing critical energy infrastructure to building responsive applications that make a difference.

Beyond academics and professional experiences, I’m a collaborative team player skilled in programming languages like C++, Python, Java, and web technologies. I’ve built projects ranging from a home security system using ESP32 to a full-stack contact manager application. My roles as Vice President of Knights Powerlifting and active memberships in IEEE and NSBE demonstrate my commitment to leadership and community engagement.

I'm driven to contribute to the fields of embedded software, automation, and systems engineering, bringing creativity and a solution-oriented mindset to every opportunity.
